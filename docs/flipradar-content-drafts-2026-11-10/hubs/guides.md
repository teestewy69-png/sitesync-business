Practical guides for the decisions that make or break a flip: what the house is worth, what to offer, what the work and the money really cost, and what you keep. Each guide includes a hypothetical worked example and links to the matching calculator. Free, no signup.

## Key takeaways

- **Start with value and price:** ARV, the 70% rule and your Maximum Purchase Price.
- **Then cost:** rehab line items, holding costs and financing.
- **Then strategy and outcome:** sourcing, flip vs BRRRR, what flippers actually make, and taxes.

## How to use the guides

Read the value and price guides before you make offers. Read the cost guides before you commit to a budget or a lender. Use the strategy guides when you are deciding what kind of investor to be.

## FAQ

### Are the guides free?

Yes. No account, no signup, nothing to buy.

### Where should a first-time flipper start?

With the ARV guide, then the 70% rule and Maximum Purchase Price guides.

### Do the guides use real data?

Where a guide cites outside data, it links the original source and the date accessed. Worked examples are hypothetical.

### Are the guides financial or tax advice?

No. They are educational. Check numbers with your own comps, contractors, lender, title company and CPA.

### How do the guides connect to the calculators?

Each guide links to the calculator that does its math, and the worked examples use the same sample deal.

### How often are the guides updated?

Guides with outside data note the date the source was accessed. Check the original source for newer figures.

### Is there a guide for my city?

Not currently. The guides explain how to replace illustrative numbers with local comps, bids and costs.
