Six free calculators for underwriting a flip or a BRRRR before you make an offer. Each page shows the formula, explains every input, and works through a hypothetical example. No account, no signup, nothing to buy.

## Key takeaways

- **Use them in order:** ARV, then a quick screen, then rehab, financing and profit.
- **Every calculator uses only the numbers you enter.** Local comps, bids and loan quotes make the result useful.
- **Each page includes a worked example on the same sample house,** so you can follow one deal through all six tools.

## The calculators

### [ARV calculator](/tools/arv-calculator)

Enter the subject's square footage and three sold comps. The calculator normalizes them by price per square foot and returns an ARV. The page explains how to adjust comps before you enter them, how to choose comps by date and distance, and how to report ARV as a range.

### [70% rule calculator](/tools/70-percent-rule-calculator)

Enter ARV, rehab and a rule percentage to get a maximum allowable offer. The page shows 65%, 70% and 75% side by side, explains why to tighten the percentage in a hot market and loosen it only when the deal still protects your margin, and covers wholesale fees and negative results.

### [Rehab cost estimator](/tools/rehab-cost-estimator)

Price the kitchen, baths, big-ticket systems and finishes, then add a contingency. The page includes illustrative cost ranges by scope and explains how to replace them with your own contractor bids.

### [Flip profit calculator](/tools/flip-profit-calculator)

Enter ARV, purchase, rehab, months held, monthly holding cost and closing costs to see net profit, return on total cost and margin. The page adds break-even sale price, annualized return and a stress test for a longer hold and a lower sale price.

### [Hard money loan calculator](/tools/hard-money-loan-calculator)

Enter the loan amount, rate, points, months and fees to see total financing cost. The page explains loan-to-cost and loan-to-ARV limits, draws, extensions, and why interest on drawn funds costs less than interest on the full balance.

### [BRRRR calculator](/tools/brrrr-calculator)

Enter purchase, rehab, ARV, refinance terms, rent and operating costs to see cash left in the deal, monthly cash flow and cash-on-cash return. The page adds lender and investor DSCR, seasoning rules and capital recovery.

## Worked example (hypothetical)

The sample house used on every calculator page: 1,500 sq ft, 3 bed / 2 bath, asking $185,000, ARV $295,000, rehab $70,000 with contingency, bought for $145,000 with a hard money loan, sold after six months. Net profit is about $37,300. At nine months with a 5% lower sale price, it is about $14,300.

## FAQ

### Are the calculators free?

Yes. No account, no signup, nothing to buy.

### Which calculator comes first?

The ARV calculator. Every other number depends on it.

### Do I need all six?

For a flip, use ARV, 70% rule, rehab, hard money (if financed) and flip profit. For a rental, add the BRRRR calculator.

### Where do the default numbers come from?

Defaults and examples are hypothetical. Replace them with your local comps, bids and quotes.

### Can I save my results?

There are no accounts, so keep your own record of your inputs and results, for example in a spreadsheet or a screenshot.

### How do I find my maximum offer, not just the 70% number?

Use the [Maximum Purchase Price guide](/guides/maximum-purchase-price) with your results from the rehab, hard money and flip profit calculators.

The 70% rule page explains how to subtract a wholesale fee from the maximum allowable offer.
